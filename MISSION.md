# Mission: Database System Design

## Why
Database System Design ke syllabus ko end-semester exam ke liye mazbooti se samajhna, taaki concepts ko apne shabdon mein explain kar sako aur syllabus ke SQL, design, transaction aur NoSQL exercises solve kar sako.

## Success looks like
- Har syllabus topic ko examples ke saath clearly samjha pao.
- SQL, normalization, transaction schedules aur MongoDB ke syllabus-based problems khud solve kar pao.

## Constraints
- Strict Hinglish mein padhai; syllabus ka order follow hoga.
- Ek topic/subtopic ek waqt par; samajh confirm hone par hi complete mark hoga.

## Out of scope
- Syllabus ke bahar ke advanced topics, jab tak kisi prerequisite ko samjhane ke liye zaroori na ho.
