Topic 03 Unit 01 / Database systems Prepared 27 Sep 2026

START HERE / DATA IN THE REAL WORLD

Database systems kahan use hote hain?

Database-system application kisi aise real-world field ya service ko kehte hain jahan data ko store, retrieve aur update karne ki zarurat hoti hai. Har field ka data alag hai—bank transactions, airline bookings ya hospital records—but in sab ko organized tareeke se manage karna padta hai.

Pichhle topic mein humne database system ke purposes dekhe the. Yahan un purposes ko different industries ke examples ke through pehchanenge.

01 / MONEY AND ACCOUNTS

Banking and Finance

Banks bahut saara customer aur financial data manage karte hain. Database mein customer, account, balance, transactions aur loans ke records ho sakte hain.

Jab ATM se ₹5,000 withdraw kiye jaate hain, system account balance check karta hai, balance update karta hai aur transaction record karta hai.

02 / FLIGHTS AND SEATS

Airline Reservation Systems

Airline reservation database passenger details, flight details, seat availability, reservations, ticket information aur payment details manage karta hai.

Flight AI-101 mein seat 24A pehle available ho sakti hai. Passenger use book kare, toh database update hota hai aur seat status Booked ho jata hai.

03 / LEARNING AND RECORDS

Universities and Colleges

College database student aur faculty records, courses, attendance, examinations, marks, fees aur library records manage kar sakta hai.

Student
  ├── Personal details
  ├── Courses
  ├── Attendance
  ├── Marks
  └── Fees

04 / SHOPPING AND ORDERS

Sales and E-Commerce

Amazon aur Flipkart jaise systems mein databases customers, products, orders, payments, delivery aur reviews ka data manage karte hain.

Customer product search karta hai, order place karta hai, payment hoti hai aur order delivery ke liye process hota hai. In steps se related records database mein manage hote hain.

05 / MAKING AND STOCKING GOODS

Manufacturing

Manufacturing companies products, raw materials, suppliers, production records, employees aur inventory se juda data store kar sakti hain.

Raw material → Production → Finished product → Inventory

06 / EMPLOYEE RECORDS

Human Resources

Company ka HR system employee information maintain karta hai, jisse employee-related records manage aur retrieve kiye ja sakte hain.

Employee
  ├── Employee ID and name
  ├── Department and salary
  ├── Attendance
  └── Leave records

07 / CALLS AND CONNECTIVITY

Telecommunications

Telecom companies customers, mobile numbers, call records, data usage, recharge aur billing information manage karti hain.

Customer ke mobile number se calls, SMS aur internet usage ke records judte hain; usage information billing mein kaam aati hai.

08 / PATIENT CARE

Healthcare and Hospitals

Hospital database patient ki personal details, medical history, doctor, tests, medicines aur bills se jude records manage karta hai. Doctor patient ka previous record retrieve kar sakta hai.

09 / PUBLIC SERVICES

Government

Government organizations citizen records, tax records, passport information, vehicle registration, electoral records aur government schemes se related data manage kar sakti hain.

THE COMMON THREAD

In applications ko DBMS kyun chahiye?

In fields mein data aam taur par large, complex, frequently changing aur shared hota hai. Isliye sirf simple files rakhna kaafi nahi hota: data store, retrieve, update aur secure karna padta hai. DBMS in needs ko organized way mein support karta hai.

  2. Airline Reservation: flights, passengers, seats aur bookings.

  4. Sales and E-Commerce: customers, products aur orders.
  5. Manufacturing: production, inventory aur suppliers.
  6. Human Resources: employee, salary aur attendance records.
  7. Telecommunications: customers, calls, data usage aur billing.
  8. Healthcare: patients, medical records aur billing.
  9. Government: citizen, tax aur administrative records.
