COURSE CONTENT / 5 UNITS

Software Engineering and Project Management syllabus

Neeche syllabus.txt ke units wahi sequence mein hain. Mid-sem QB Unit 1, 2 aur 3 se hai.

UNIT 01 Introduction to Software Engineering, Software Process Models 07 Hrs

Fundamentals and process models

Nature of Software, Principles, Software Process, Myths; Generic Process Model; Prescriptive Models: Waterfall, Incremental (RAD), Evolutionary, Unified Process, Concurrent; Agile methods, Plan-driven vs agile, Extreme Programming (practices, testing, pair programming); JIRA, Kanban; Case studies.

UNIT 02 Software Requirements Engineering and Analysis 07 Hrs

Requirements and SRS

User and system requirements; Functional and non-functional requirements; Types and metrics; Spiral view of RE; SRS document, structure, writing styles (insulin pump case study); Elicitation, analysis, validation, management.

UNIT 03 Design Engineering 07 Hrs

Design and modeling

Design process and quality, concepts, model, pattern-based design; Architectural design (decisions, views, patterns); Component-level design; UI design (golden rules, steps, evaluation); UML and diagram types; Software testing and types.

UNIT 04 Project Management: Process, Metrics, Estimations and Risks 07 Hrs

Management and estimation

Management spectrum, W5HH, metrics, FP and LOC measurement; Estimation, planning, scope, resources; Decomposition, COCOMO II, object-oriented estimation; Scheduling, task networks, time-line charts, tracking tools.

UNIT 05 Project Management: Risk, Configuration, Maintenance and Reengineering 07 Hrs

Risk and evolution

Risk management (reactive vs proactive, identification, projection, mitigation, RMMM); SCM repository and process; WebApps configuration; Maintenance, supportability, reengineering, reverse engineering, restructuring, forward engineering.
