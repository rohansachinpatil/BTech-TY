COURSE CONTENT / 5 UNITS

Theory of Computations syllabus

Neeche syllabus.txt ke units wahi sequence mein hain. Mid-sem QB mainly Unit 1, Unit 2 aur Unit 3 ke shuruati concepts se hai.

UNIT 01 Formal Language Theory and Finite Automata 07 Hrs

Basics and automata

Basic Mathematical Objects: Sets, Logic, Functions, Relations; Formal language, Alphabets and languages, Finite representation of language; Finite Automata: informal picture, Finite State Machine, Language accepted by FA, Regular Language definition; DFA and NFA, epsilon-NFA; FA with output: Moore and Mealy machines — definition, models, inter-conversion; Application of FA: Text Search.

UNIT 02 Regular Expressions (RE) and Languages 07 Hrs

RE and conversions

Operators of RE, Building RE, Precedence of operators, Algebraic laws for RE; Conversions: NFA to DFA, RE to DFA, DFA to RE, State/loop elimination, Arden’s theorem; Properties: Pumping Lemma, Closure and Decision properties; Applications: RE in UNIX, Lexical analysis, Finding patterns in text.

UNIT 03 Context Free Grammars (CFG) and Languages 07 Hrs

Grammars and parsing

Introduction, Regular Grammar, CFG definition, Derivations, Language of a grammar, Sentential forms, Parse trees; Ambiguity; Simplification of CFG; Normal Forms: Chomsky normal form, Greibach normal form; Closure and Decision properties; Chomsky Hierarchy; Applications: Parsers, YACC Parser-Generator, Markup languages, XML and Document Type Definitions.

UNIT 04 Pushdown Automata (PDA) and Linear Bounded Automata (LBA) 07 Hrs

Stack and bounded memory

PDA definition, Languages of a PDA, Acceptance by final state and empty stack, Equivalence of PDA and CFG, Deterministic PDA; LBA definition, Language of LBA, LBA and Context Sensitive Language.

UNIT 05 Turing Machines (TM) 07 Hrs

Computability

Problems that computers cannot solve; TM notation, Instantaneous description, Transition diagrams, Language of TM, TM and Halting; Programming techniques for TM; Extensions to basic TM; TM and Computers; Church-Turing Thesis; Universal TM.
